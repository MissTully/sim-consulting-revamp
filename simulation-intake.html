What you walk away with

One call. A clear plan for simulation in your program.

This is a working session, not a sales pitch. We look at what you teach and what you have, and you leave the call with three things:

A realistic starting point

Where to begin with the equipment, space, and time you actually have. No lab required: simulation can start with role play, task trainers, or screen-based encounters.

Scenario ideas at your learners' level

Concrete scenarios matched to who you teach: vitals and reporting changes for CNAs and PCTs, rooming and intake for MAs, med passes and SBAR handoffs for LPNs and RNs.

A debrief you can run on day one

A simple, evidence-based debrief structure so your first simulation teaches instead of just happening. This is where most of the learning lives, and where new sim educators need the most support.
