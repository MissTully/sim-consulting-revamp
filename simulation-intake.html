New to teaching with simulation? Start here.

This intake is built for educators who are early in their teaching journey, whether you train CNAs, PCTs, MAs, LPNs, or RNs. You do not need a simulation lab, a manikin, or years of experience to begin. Built for first-time sim educators

Every question below assumes you may be brand new to simulation. If you have never written a scenario or led a debrief, that is exactly who this intake is for.

Every level of learner

CNA, PCT, MA, LPN/LVN, and RN programs all benefit from simulation, from bed baths and vitals to SBAR handoffs and med passes. Your starter plan is matched to the learners you actually teach.

Start with what you have

No dedicated equipment yet? Simulation can start with role play, standardized patients, task trainers, or screen-based encounters. We meet you at your current resources, not an ideal lab.
